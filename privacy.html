Lunara Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

Lunara respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and health-related data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Lunara app.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal information (such as email, if you create an account)
  • Health and cycle data (such as period dates, symptoms, moods, and cycle history)
  • Usage data (such as app interactions and preferences)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide cycle tracking and predictions
  • Improve app performance and features
  • Personalize your experience
  • Send reminders and notifications (if enabled)

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal or health data.

We may use trusted third-party services (such as analytics or cloud storage) to operate the app, but only as necessary to provide our services.

4. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data using secure storage and encryption practices. However, no system is completely secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data

To request deletion, contact us at: your@email.com

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations.

7. Children’s Privacy

Lunara is not intended for users under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your region).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
